Clipper

A personal tool for posting my own gaming stream clips.

Clipper is a private, self-hosted tool run by one person on their own computer. It finds highlights in livestreams from creators whose official clipping campaigns I have joined, turns them into short vertical clips, and lets me review each clip. Only after I approve a clip and click Upload does Clipper post it to my own TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, with a caption I write.

Clipper is not offered to the public. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it doesn't collect information about anyone.
